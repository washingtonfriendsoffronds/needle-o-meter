Useless statistics

Space Needle: 47.6205° N, 122.3493° W · 605 ft tall · base about 164 ft above sea level, so the tip is about 769 ft. The base elevation and preset elevations are approximate.

GPS altitude is often off by 30 ft or more, and some phones report height above the ellipsoid rather than sea level (around Seattle that reads roughly 75 ft low). Proceed with appropriate unseriousness.
